# Faith O. Oyedemi

**Research Software Developer · Quantum Simulation · Machine Learning**

Ottawa, Ontario, Canada ·
[Email](mailto:faithoyedemi@gmail.com) ·
[GitHub](https://github.com/lere01)

I am a physics researcher and software developer working at the intersection
of quantum many-body systems, machine learning, and scientific computing. My
current research uses neural-network quantum states to model complex physical
systems and compares their predictions with exact diagonalization and other
numerical benchmarks.

I am particularly interested in computational methods that make scientific
results more accurate, reproducible, and efficient. That work includes neural
quantum states, Monte Carlo methods, high-performance computing, deterministic
experiment design, and scientific software in Python and Rust. My interest in
**neuronal dynamics** reflects a parallel interest in spiking neural networks
and energy-efficient computation.

I am interested in research software, scientific computing, quantum
technology, AI/ML, HPC, defence technology, and other deep-technology
environments where careful modelling and reliable software must work together.

## Selected technical work

### qslib

A Rust and Python family of quantum-simulation libraries covering
stochastic-series-expansion quantum Monte Carlo, variational diagnostics, and
deterministic experiment seeding. The packages are distributed through
crates.io and PyPI.

[Guide](https://lere01.github.io/qslib/) ·
[Source](https://github.com/lere01/qslib) ·
[crates.io](https://crates.io/crates/qslib-quantum) ·
[PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/qslib-quantum/)

### SSE

A reproducible stochastic-series-expansion simulator for transverse-field
Ising and Rydberg lattices, with a Rust command-line interface, documented
run manifests, and a WebAssembly demonstration that runs in the browser.

[Guide](https://lere01.github.io/sse/) ·
[WebAssembly demo](https://lere01.github.io/sse/demo/) ·
[Source](https://github.com/lere01/sse)
